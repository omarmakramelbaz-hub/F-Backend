# Branch shift reports

Only the persisted central owner and administrative admin (`account_type=admin`, no `owner_resturant_id`, with dashboard order access) can open, close, recover or reprint shifts. Branch accounts cannot access that workflow or its direct endpoints. The branch print receiver has separate, claim-scoped access to the exact report management sent it.

Closing saves an immutable report and source claims, and queues one branch print job in the same transaction. It does not debit or reset the POS till, insert a cash movement, change its revision, or replace expected cash with the counted amount. Expected cash carries into the next shift, including earlier app cash and delivery adjustments. Later shifts report newly claimed sales/expenses once. Historical reports and genuine legacy resets remain unchanged.

Expected cash = carried opening cash + new POS till movements + newly recognized app cash collected by the branch − newly collected POS delivery fees. Cash expenses already affect till movements and are not deducted twice. The receipt shows actual cash, expected cash and the difference.

The management page queues printing; it never invokes its local printer. The selected branch's existing dashboard receiver polls the shift queue, atomically claims each job and prints through `DashboardPrint` on that branch device. Its dashboard must be running and connected, with direct printing configured. Pending jobs wait for reconnection. Claimed/failed jobs are not automatically recycled after an uncertain physical invocation; management can explicitly send another copy. Completion means the print invocation was acknowledged, not proof of paper output.

After each successful close, each channel (takeaway, dining, phone delivery and app) has a separate new branch sequence beginning at 1. Numbers are stored when new orders are created; editing or paying an older ticket preserves its number. A branch-row lock serializes allocation with closing. The displayed prefix includes branch, channel and shift (`TA100-S2-0001`, `DI100-S2-0001`, `PH100-S2-0001`, `R100-S2-0001`), so successive shifts remain distinguishable. App `order_no` is assigned once and used by the existing customer API and dashboard. Database IDs and earlier order numbers are never renumbered. The new policy activates after the branch's first close; deployment does not bulk-renumber existing data.

`shift_report_integration_test.php` uses real services, a real Order model and disposable SQLite tables to cover cash carry, source deduplication, roles, forged actors, job capabilities, retries, rollback on queue failure, legacy reset compatibility and per-channel sequences. `browser/shift-report-routing.cjs` checks management enqueueing and branch-device printing.

The pinned installer checks every source before applying narrow three-way patches, saves private backups, rejects conflicting live changes and adds only the specific idempotent schema extension. It does not execute unrelated pending migrations or rewrite historical balances.
