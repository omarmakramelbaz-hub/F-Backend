# Branch operations rollout

The pinned installer applies `2026_10_04_080000_create_branch_operations.php` after the existing POS migrations. No existing customer, salary, cash or restaurant-price data is rewritten or backfilled.

- **Customers:** branch-scoped saved contacts, app customers authorized for the actor, and recent phone-order contacts. Confirming delivery details saves the contact before choosing products. Egyptian national/international, Arabic and Persian digits match; app accounts are never modified through this directory. The central administrator can choose any authorized branch, while branch users keep their existing scope.
- **Delivery companies:** create, edit or deactivate per branch. Select an active company in customer details; editing an unpaid/unissued ticket allows changing the company. The ticket and kitchen/payment snapshots retain its recorded name and contact. The company page opens its assigned orders. This is assignment/counting, not an automatic carrier API or carrier settlement.
- **Employees:** daily attendance, shift/job filters and dated bonuses, deductions and advances. Monthly base salary has an effective month; hire/leave dates prorate by calendar days. Attendance does not automatically invent deduction amounts. Closing a statement freezes its snapshot and locks that month's attendance/entries. Recording payment requires confirmation and cannot duplicate payment. Negative net dues are shown without automatic payment or carry-forward. Payroll tracking does not change the POS till; approved branch expenses remain the cash-ledger workflow. CSV export is Excel-compatible and protects against formula injection.
- **Invoices:** read-only details remain usable across SPA navigation. Sending kitchen updates is separate from issuing the orange payment bill. Issued bills remain locked. Collection never invokes printing for dining or phone orders; reprinting remains an explicit action.
- **Maps:** see `dashboard_maps.md`. Pin selection has a Google-independent path. Google address search requires authorized Maps JavaScript and Geocoding APIs/billing.
- **Shift closing:** see `branch_shifts.md`. Branch sales are split by dining, takeaway, phone delivery and app, less delivery fees and approved expenses. All accounts enter a blind cash count; sales totals, expected cash and variance appear only on the saved closing's printed paper. Closing does not withdraw or transfer money.

Validation checklist: create a saved customer without an order, search by a phone prefix, choose an allowed branch, confirm its customer pin and fee, assign a company, choose menu items and send once. Verify the branch print receiver sees the job. Issue a payment bill, verify editing is locked, then collect without a new print job. Create an employee, record attendance/entries, review and close the period, then record payment once. Verify another branch cannot read or modify those records.

Server rollout, live Google authorization and physical paper output require the production server/branch device; a successful browser print invocation is not proof of physical printing. Continue using the existing branch Chrome direct-print launcher.

October 4 design follow-up: the employee grid now reports the selected **day**, with independent red deduction, green bonus and amber advance cells. Each cell exposes its count/amount, a dedicated add form (reason/type select, amount and notes), and a read-only detail table with Cairo time, reason, amount and notes. Voided entries stay in the monthly audit history and are excluded from daily totals. Filters apply consistently to summary cards and footer totals. Monthly payroll remains a separate action, preserving closed snapshots and existing free-text reasons. Daily notes are saved with the attendance revision; uncertain financial writes reuse the original command key.

Employee entry cancellation is restricted to the installation's primary owner (persisted admin user ID 1, Fasakhaninja). Branch operators, restaurant owners and other administrative accounts cannot void advances, deductions or bonuses, even by a direct request. The statement reports this permission separately, so the action is absent for other users. Owner cancellation still requires a reason, the current revision and an open payroll month; it preserves the audit row and supports exact replay.
